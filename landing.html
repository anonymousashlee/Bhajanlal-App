How it works

Three steps to a feature you can defend in review.

Connect your data

Point Bhajanlal at tickets, docs, or logs. We dedupe, redact PII, and split a held-out test set you never train on.

Define what “good” means

Pick metrics and thresholds — accuracy, refusal rate, tone, latency. Every change must clear the gate to ship.

Ship and keep watching

Deploy with one click. Dashboards flag drift, and failures flow back into the next curation round.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need my own eval dataset to start?

No. We bootstrap a starter set from your connected data, then you refine the rubric as your product definition of “good” sharpens.

Which models does Bhajanlal support?

Any major hosted or open-weights model. You define the routing policy — cost, latency, or quality — and we handle failover.

How does the learning loop handle user data?

Feedback is redacted, deduplicated, and reviewed against your retention policy before it re-enters a training or few-shot set. Nothing moves silently.

Can I self-host?

Yes — Enterprise plans deploy inside your own cloud account with the same pipeline and dashboards.
