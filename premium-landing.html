FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I bring my own model weights?

Yes — import any Hugging Face or local checkpoint and run evals before deploying.

How does billing work?

Pay per seat plus usage-based inference. Hard spend caps prevent surprise bills.

Is my data used for training?

No. Your data stays in your tenant and is never used to train shared models.

Enterprise plans include an on-prem deployment option with full feature parity.
