Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know to get started.

How do I get started?
Sign up for a free account, connect your data source, and launch your first training run in under 5 minutes. No credit card required.
Can I use my own models?
Yes. Bhajanlal supports PyTorch, TensorFlow, and Hugging Face models. Bring your own code or use our templates.
Is my data secure?
Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We are SOC 2 Type II certified and never use your data for training our own models.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, cancel anytime with no penalties. Your data remains accessible for 30 days after cancellation.
